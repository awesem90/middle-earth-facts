Middle-earth Facts: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Middle-earth Facts is an offline app that shows one Tolkien fact per day.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device information.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your favourite facts and your reminder settings (on/off and time) on your own device only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily reminder locally on your device. No server is involved.

Sharing

When you tap “Share”, the fact text is handed to the Android share sheet and sent only to the app you choose.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: treefolkinteractive@gmail.com

Middle-earth Facts is an unofficial fan app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Tolkien Estate, Middle-earth Enterprises, HarperCollins, Warner Bros., New Line Cinema or Amazon.
